# ADR index

Put architecture decision records here as `NNNN-title.md`.

Example:

```markdown
# ADR 0001: <short decision title>

## Status
Proposed | Accepted | Superseded

## Context
Why a decision is needed for this repository.

## Decision
What we chose (stack, ports layout, ownership carve-outs, etc.).

## Consequences
What becomes easier or harder; what to revisit later.
```

Do not copy starter-product decisions from another repo — record decisions for **this** target only.
